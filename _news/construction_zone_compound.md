Glen Whitney, the founder of [Studio Infinity](https://studioinfinity.org), visited Louisiana Tech University to lead the construction of a large-scale mathematical art installation on campus during the First-Year Projects Showcase.

---

### A mathematical structure at architectural scale
The installation, a compound of five tetrahedra, displays the symmetry of one of the Platonic solids.
It was built collaboratively on Thursday and disassembled on Friday morning, and it brought together students, faculty, and visitors in a hands-on exploration of three-dimensional geometry at architectural scale.

---

### Mathematics as a participatory experience
Studio Infinity’s work treats mathematics as a physical, collaborative activity.
By constructing temporary, large-scale installations, their projects make abstract mathematical ideas tangible and invite participants to experience mathematics by building it together.
